# Catchment coverage reconciliation

**Verified:** 2026-09-14 against the current ECan inventories and the fresh
2007–2025 profile. This is source-inventory reconciliation, not a claim of
complete environmental monitoring.

The deterministic audit is generated by:

```bash
python3 tools/reconcile_catchment_coverage.py \
  --profile reports/generated/ashburton-analytical-profile-2007-2025-all-sites.json \
  --site-audit reports/generated/ashburton-catchment-site-coverage-2026-09-14.json \
  --output reports/generated/ashburton-catchment-coverage-reconciliation.json
```

The 321 unmatched in-bound surface features are retained in the audit because

sites are excluded by the verified polygon; no coordinate-bearing Hilltop rows
were missing coordinates.

## Known source limits and assumptions

- ArcGIS completeness is checked through the service count, ordered pages,
  page sizes, response feature counts, metadata spatial reference, and transfer
  limit handling. The boundary parser rejects a transfer-limited or multi-
  feature response and includes points on the polygon boundary.
- Hilltop completeness means all coordinate-bearing `SiteList` rows returned by
  the public service at retrieval time. It does not prove that an unlisted or
  non-coordinate site exists.
- The observation reconciliation covers the selected parameters and requested
  2007–2025 dates. It cannot prove that absent unrequested parameters or dates
  do not exist upstream.
- Source inventories can contain historical, duplicated, retired, or
  non-water-quality spatial features. Exact stable identifiers are therefore
  required before an observation site is included in the analytical station
  scope.
